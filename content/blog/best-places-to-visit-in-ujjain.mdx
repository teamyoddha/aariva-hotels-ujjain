Ujjain brings together temples, riverside ghats and places of cultural interest. Choose the stops that matter to your group and leave time to rest between outings.

## Temples and sacred places

Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlinga, Harsiddhi Temple and Kal Bhairav Temple are among the places visitors often include in their trip. Sandipani Ashram is another place to consider when planning your route.

## Mahakal Lok and Ram Ghat

Explore the sculptures and surroundings of Mahakal Lok, or spend time by the Shipra at Ram Ghat. Confirm current access and visiting arrangements before setting out.

## Planning your day

Check temple timings, entry requirements and any booking arrangements directly with the relevant authorities. Travel times vary with traffic and festival crowds; use current maps for directions from the hotel.
