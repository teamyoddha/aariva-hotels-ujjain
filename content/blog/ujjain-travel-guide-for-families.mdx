Ujjain is a wonderful city to visit as a family. Grandparents come for darshan, children enjoy the stories behind each temple, and evenings by the river are something everyone remembers.

Travelling as a group does need a little more planning. Here is what we have learnt from welcoming families at Aariva.

## Pace your days

The most common mistake is trying to see everything in one day. With children or elders, aim for:

- **One major temple in the morning**, when everyone is fresh.
- **A long rest in the afternoon**, especially from April to June.
- **A relaxed evening outing**, such as the aarti at Ram Ghat or a walk through Mahakal Lok.

Our [2-day itinerary](/blog/2-day-ujjain-travel-itinerary/) follows this pattern.

## Handling temple crowds

- **Choose quieter times.** Weekday mornings are usually calmer than evenings, weekends and Mondays.
- **Agree on a meeting point** before entering any temple, and make sure children know it.
- **Write your phone number** on a card in your child's pocket.
- **Ask about facilities for elders.** Mahakaleshwar has had arrangements for elderly and differently-abled devotees. Ask at the help desk about current options.
- **Carry water and light snacks**, and keep a small bag with essentials.

## Places children enjoy

- **Sandipani Ashram**, where Krishna is said to have studied. Children love hearing that story.
- **Shri Mahakal Lok**, with its large sculptures and open walkways, especially in the evening.
- **Ram Ghat** at sunset for the aarti and lamps on the water.
- **Vedh Shala (Jantar Mantar)**, an old observatory that older children find fascinating.

## Getting around with family

For a group, a cab for the day is usually the most comfortable choice. It saves bargaining for each trip and gives elders a place to rest between stops. Autos and e-rickshaws are handy for short hops within the old city.

## Packing list for families

- Photo ID for every adult
- Comfortable, modest clothing and easy slip-on footwear
- A light shawl or jacket for winter mornings
- Water bottles, sun hats and basic medicines
- A small bag for footwear tokens, prasad and keys
